Privacy

Privacy Policy for www.clairo.care

This page explains what information this website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language on purpose. Effective September 2, 2026.

What we collect Advertising and Meta Cookies Your choices

Section 01

Who we are and what this covers

Who runs this site

Clairo Care provides home and community-based services for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities in Maryland (DDA) and Pennsylvania (ODP). This website, www.clairo.care, is our public site for families and caregivers who want to learn about us or get in touch. You can reach us at hello@clairo.care or (240) 621-1353.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the public website only. If you or a family member receives services from Clairo Care, the way we handle your health information is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices under HIPAA, which we provide when services begin. Our staff and caregiver application, which requires a login, has its own terms.

The short version

We collect what you choose to give us through the Talk to Clairo form, and the basic technical information any website receives. We use a Meta advertising pixel only if you accept it, and we never send Meta what you type into our forms. We do not sell your information.

Section 02

What we collect and why

Information you give us

When you submit the Talk to Clairo form, we receive what you enter: your name, email, phone number, county, whether you are interested in traditional or self-directed services, which services you are asking about, whether you already have a caregiver in mind, and anything you write in the notes. We use it to respond to you, to understand whether Clairo may be a fit, and, if you choose to move forward, to begin setting up services. Anything you tell us about a person's care needs is treated as confidential health information: it is stored in our care-coordination system, and only Clairo staff who need it to respond to you can see it.

Information collected automatically

Like every website, ours receives technical information when you visit: your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view, and the site that referred you. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this information to identify you.

Calls and text messages

If you give us a phone number, we may call or text you about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop at any time, and agreeing to be contacted is never a condition of receiving services.

Section 03

Advertising and the Meta Pixel

What the Meta Pixel is

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram, which are run by Meta Platforms, Inc. To understand whether those ads reach families and to measure them, this site can use a small piece of Meta code called the Meta Pixel, together with a matching server-side signal called the Conversions API. Meta may combine what it receives with information it already holds about you if you have a Facebook or Instagram account.

It is off until you accept

The pixel does not load, set cookies, or send anything to Meta unless you click Accept on the banner at the bottom of the page. If you click Decline, or never answer, Meta receives nothing from this site. Your choice is saved on your own device and you can change it any time using the Cookie preferences link in the footer.

What Meta receives if you accept

The pages you view on this site, your IP address and browser type, two Meta cookie identifiers (described under Cookies), and the fact that a contact form was submitted, if you submit one. That is all.

What Meta never receives

Your name, email address, phone number, county, the services you asked about, or anything you type into a form on this site. We have built the site so that the form contents cannot be sent to Meta, and we do not use Meta's Advanced Matching feature, which would send hashed contact details. We also send Meta a Limited Data Use instruction with every signal, which tells Meta to apply the processing limits of your state's privacy law.

Controls Meta offers

You can review and limit how Meta uses information from websites in your Meta ad settings, and read Meta's Privacy Policy for how Meta handles data it receives.

Section 04

Cookies and similar storage

Your consent choice

When you answer the banner, we save your choice in your browser's local storage under the name clairo_consent_v1 so we do not ask again. It contains only the word "granted" or "denied" and the date. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Meta cookies, only if you accept

_fbp is a random identifier Meta uses to recognize the same browser across visits. _fbc is set only if you arrived by clicking one of our ads, and records the click so Meta can connect it to a later visit or form submission. Both are set by Meta's code, last up to 90 days, and are not created if you decline.

No other tracking

We do not use other analytics, advertising, or session-recording tools on this site. If that changes, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Section 05

Sharing, security, and retention

Who we share information with

Service providers that host this website and run our care-coordination and email systems, who may only use the information to provide those services to us. Meta, as described above, and only with your consent. Government agencies or others when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How we protect it

Form submissions travel over an encrypted connection and are stored in systems with access limited to staff who need them. No website can promise perfect security, and we will tell you if a problem affects your information as the law requires.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information as long as we are in contact with you about services, and afterward only as long as our record-keeping obligations require. If you ask us to delete your inquiry and we have no legal duty to keep it, we will.

Children

This site is meant for adults. Parents and guardians often tell us about a child's care needs, and we handle that information as described above. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

Section 06

Your choices and how to reach us

Change your tracking choice

Use the Cookie preferences link in the footer of any page to accept or decline the Meta Pixel. Declining takes effect right away for future page views.

See, correct, or delete your information

Email hello@clairo.care and tell us what you would like. We will confirm who you are and respond within 45 days. Residents of Maryland, Pennsylvania, and other states may have specific rights under state law; we honor these requests for everyone, wherever you live. If we cannot fulfill a request, for example because we must keep a record, we will explain why.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change means sending more information to an advertising partner, we will ask for your consent again before it takes effect.

Contact

Clairo Care, hello@clairo.care, (240) 621-1353. Questions about this policy or about how we handle your information are welcome.
