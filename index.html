Addressing the SDS changes

Concerned about caregiver wages and the 60/40 rule?

Changes to Maryland Self-Directed Services have left many families unsure how much they will be able to pay caregivers. Clairo's low-overhead model is designed to put more of the service rate directly into caregiver compensation.

For some families, moving certain services to Clairo may help preserve competitive caregiver wages while keeping other services self-directed.
