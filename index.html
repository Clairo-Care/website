Frequently asked questions

A few things families usually want to know.

Is Clairo a traditional provider or Self-Directed Services?

Clairo is a provider agency for the services Clairo delivers. Our model is designed to preserve many of the things families value — caregiver choice, strong compensation, flexibility, and participant involvement — while Clairo handles provider administration.

Can my current caregiver work through Clairo?

Often, existing caregivers can apply to become Clairo employees, subject to service requirements, qualifications, onboarding, and the participant's individual circumstances.

Do I have to leave SDS completely to use Clairo?

Not necessarily. Depending on the participant's plan, services, authorizations, and DDA requirements, selected services may be delivered by Clairo while other applicable services remain self-directed.

Does Clairo handle payroll and compliance?

For services delivered by Clairo, Clairo handles its associated employment, payroll, training, billing, compliance, and provider responsibilities.
