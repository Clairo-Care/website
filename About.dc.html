Why we exist

Clairo Care exists because the traditional model wasn't working

We help you care for those with I/DD without losing sight of you.

Our mission

Too much funding meant for care was getting eaten up by overhead, while caregivers — the people doing the hardest, most important work — were underpaid and unsupported. At the same time, the caregiver staffing crisis has made it harder for families to find and hire trusted staff, and some providers still hesitate to hire family members at all.

Our plan

Instead of layering on overhead like a traditional provider, we built a technology-first company that automates the administrative work that used to require large teams. That efficiency means more of every dollar goes to the people actually providing care.

At the end of the day, Clairo Care exists because families deserve better support, caregivers deserve to be valued, and we believe technology can help deliver both.
