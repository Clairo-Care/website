Beyond the hourly rate

Support that makes the work sustainable.

Competitive wages

Personal Supports $32.50–$35.50/hr; Community Development Services $40+/hr possible, depending on allocated funding.

Mileage reimbursement

Eligible travel is reimbursed, so supporting community participation does not come out of your pocket.

Health benefits

Available for eligible caregivers under the applicable plan terms.

Paid time off

Available for eligible caregivers — because rest is part of doing this work well.

Training and certifications

Required training is supported and tracked, with expirations flagged before they lapse.

Real support

A named coordinator who knows your participant, plus tools that make notes and hours simple.

Wages vary by service, participant circumstances, available service funding, employee eligibility, and other applicable factors. Benefits and paid time off are available to eligible employees under the applicable plan terms.

Family and friends

The person who knows them best may already be in the room.

Where DDA rules permit, qualified family members and friends may be able to provide support as Clairo employees — with the same wages, training, and administrative support as any other caregiver.

Eligibility depends on the service, applicable DDA requirements, caregiver qualifications, and the participant's individual circumstances.
