Three conceptual paths

None of these is universally better.

Which path makes sense depends entirely on the participant's plan, authorizations, services, caregivers, and priorities. Here is how each one tends to work.

Path one

Remain fully self-directed

You keep the employer role and continue directing all services as you do today. For many families this is the right answer, and nothing here suggests otherwise.

You keep full control of the employer budget
Administrative responsibilities stay with the family
No change to your current care team

Path two

Move selected applicable services to Clairo

Depending on the plan and authorizations, some services may be delivered by Clairo while other applicable services remain self-directed.

Clairo employs caregivers only for services it delivers
Other services can stay in self-direction
Administration for Clairo-delivered services moves to us

Path three

Potentially transition more services to Clairo

Some families eventually prefer to have more of their services delivered by a provider agency and step back from employer responsibilities.

Clairo carries payroll, compliance, and provider duties
Families stay involved in building the care team
Can be approached gradually, service by service

Every participant's plan, authorizations, services, and circumstances are different. Whether any service can move to a provider agency — and what happens to the rest — depends on DDA requirements and the individual plan. Clairo is funded as a provider agency for the services Clairo delivers and does not control a participant's self-directed employer budget. Nothing on this page is regulatory guidance.
