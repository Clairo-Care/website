Worried about the changes to Maryland Self-Directed Services?

There may be another way to keep the caregivers you trust — and the wages that help you keep them.

Self-direction gives your family real choice — along with a pile of administrative work, worries about DDA changes, and more. Clairo Care takes that off your plate.

Caregiver first

When we build our budget, caregiver wages come first — not last.

With Clairo Care, a caregiver's family relationship to the participant is irrelevant to their wage.

CDS

How?

We receive the same amount of money as every other DDA provider. Our tech-enabled systems and unique business model let us sustainably pay and support caregivers, and still leave enough to keep operating and keep improving.
