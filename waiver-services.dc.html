Personal Supports

Everyday and enhanced support, built around the participant.

Personal Supports is help with daily life — the routines, tasks, and activities that make independence possible. It includes enhanced support for participants whose needs call for a higher level of staffing, training, or support intensity. Support is delivered in the home and in the community, shaped by the participant's own plan and goals.

Help with daily living activities and routines
Building independence and personal skills
Participation in community life
Enhanced support for participants with more complex needs
Support toward the goals in the individual plan
Delivered one-to-one by a caregiver on the participant's team
Caregiver wages $32.50–$51/hr Ask if this service applies →

Community Development Services

A meaningful life in the community.

Community Development Services (CDS) supports participation in community life — building skills, relationships, routines, and the kind of ordinary belonging that makes a week feel like a life.

Community participation and activities
Skill building toward greater independence
Building relationships and connections
Support that follows the participant's interests and goals
Caregiver wages $40+/hr possible* Ask if this service applies →

Nursing Supports

Professional support when nursing is part of the plan.

Nursing Supports provides licensed nursing care within the participant's approved DDA services and individual plan, coordinated alongside the rest of the care team.

Delivered by licensed nursing professionals
Coordinated with the participant's wider care team
Provided within the approved individual plan
Available as authorized under DDA services
Compensation varies by role and qualifications Ask if this service applies →

EMPLOYMENT SUPPORTS *COMING SOON*

Support for meaningful work and greater independence.

Employment Supports is individualized help to explore, obtain, learn, and maintain meaningful work — matched to the participant’s interests, strengths, and goals, and coordinated with their DDA plan.

Exploring interests, strengths, and career options
Support finding and starting a job
On-the-job learning and coaching
Ongoing support to keep and grow in the role
Authorized through the participant’s DDA plan
Wages vary by service and available funding Ask if this service applies →

Which services a participant can receive, and from whom, depends on their DDA plan, authorizations, and individual circumstances. *Community Development Services wages depend on the funding allocated to that service in the participant's budget.
