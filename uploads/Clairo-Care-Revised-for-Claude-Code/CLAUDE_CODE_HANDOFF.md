# Clairo Care Homepage Revision Handoff

## Goal
Make the homepage speak first to Maryland families concerned about recent Self-Directed Services (SDS) changes, while preserving the broader Clairo Care brand and pathways for traditional Maryland services and Pennsylvania ODP visitors.

## Changes already made in the included HTML

### Homepage: `Clairo Site Wireframe.dc.html`
1. Changed the hero eyebrow to **Maryland Self-Directed Services**.
2. Changed the hero H1 to **Worried about the changes to Maryland Self-Directed Services?**
3. Kept the supporting line: **There may be another way to keep the caregivers you trust — and the wages that help you keep them.**
4. Reframed the explanatory paragraph so Clairo does not imply that Clairo itself is SDS.
5. Replaced “Learn what self-direction looks like with Clairo Care” with **See how Clairo can complement your SDS plan**.
6. Made caregiver compensation concrete in the benefit strip: wages start at $32.50/hr, with CDS potentially reaching $40+/hr, plus mileage reimbursement and eligible benefits.
7. Added a **wage snapshot section** immediately after the SDS/control section, before “How it works.”
8. Preserved the existing **You stay in control. We handle the rest.** comparison and partial-transition language.
9. Preserved the three alternate pathways for visitors not currently in Maryland SDS.

### Wages page: `Wages and Benefits.dc.html`
Replaced the phrase about SDS being “a pile of administrative work” with more neutral, pro-choice language that acknowledges the benefits of self-direction while explaining why some families may want another option.

## Important messaging guardrails
- Do not imply that Clairo Care itself is a self-directed service or SDS provider model.
- Position Clairo as an option that can **complement** a family’s SDS plan or receive selected services while other services remain self-directed, when applicable.
- Keep the tone supportive of SDS and family choice; the problem is the uncertainty created by recent program changes, not self-direction itself.
- Preserve the statement that some families may move only certain services to Clairo rather than moving everything out of SDS.
- Keep the concrete compensation figures visible and easy to scan.

## Implementation request
Please take these revised wireframe HTML files and apply the same copy, section order, hierarchy, and styling changes to the production website codebase. Preserve the existing Clairo design system, responsive behavior, accessibility, and components rather than hard-coding wireframe-only markup where production components already exist.

Before finalizing, verify desktop and mobile layouts and make sure the new wage snapshot does not create awkward wrapping at common breakpoints.
